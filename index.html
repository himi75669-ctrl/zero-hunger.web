What is Zero Hunger?

SDG 2 aims to end all forms of hunger and malnutrition by 2030, ensuring that all people — especially children and the vulnerable — have access to sufficient, safe, and nutritious food all year round.

Why Hunger Matters & The Waste Paradox

Hunger is not caused by a global lack of food. The world produces enough food to feed everyone. The primary barriers are food loss, logistics inefficiencies, uneven distribution, and commercial food waste during events and catering.

Redirecting just 15% of surplus edible food could save millions of lives and significantly slow down climate degradation caused by decomposing organic waste in landfills.
